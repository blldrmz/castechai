The approach / 01

See the table.
Understand the event.

Casino table operations depend on accuracy, speed and consistent interpretation of fast-moving events.

CasTech AI aims to turn camera observations into structured, actionable operational signals — supporting staff while keeping final decisions in human hands.

Architecture / 03

From pixels to operational context.

Camera-based observation is only the beginning. CasTech AI is being designed around a clear separation between recognition, game-state interpretation and actionable alerts.

01

Camera input

Table and game-area observation through configured camera angles.

02

Vision analysis

Detection and classification of relevant cards, chips, zones and movements.

03

Rules & verification

Event mapping and cross-checks aligned to the specific game module.

04

Operator feedback

Useful visual indicators and reviewable operational alerts.

Design principles / 05

Built around operational reality.

Our work starts with how casino tables actually operate: rules, staff workflow, accuracy requirements and integration constraints.

Assist, don't obscure

Make alerts explainable and give operators the context to verify the observation.

Accuracy before scale

Validate detection and rules logic systematically before expanding across game types.

Purpose-limited camera intelligence

Prioritize table events and operational consistency rather than unnecessary personal identification.
